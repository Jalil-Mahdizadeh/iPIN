Read on 28 September 2026: Liu et al., *PLM-interact: extending protein language models to predict protein-protein interactions*, Nature Communications 16, 9012 (2025), DOI [10.1038/s41467-025-64512-w](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64512-w).

Coverage: the complete published paper (14 PDF pages), supplementary information (12 pages; all 10 figures and 4 tables), reporting summary (3 pages), and peer-review reports and author responses (31 pages). Figures were inspected visually. Local PDFs retain the source material; `sources.json` records retrieval details and checksums. This was a literature reading with targeted source checks, not an independent reproduction.

**Main paper.** The contribution is joint encoding of protein pairs with ESM-2 and updating the encoder alongside interaction classification and masked-language objectives. Figure 2 reports cross-species AUPRs of 0.904, 0.913, 0.888, 0.706 and 0.722 for mouse, fly, worm, yeast and E. coli. On the Bernett benchmark, PLM-interact and TUnA both report AUPR 0.69 and AUROC 0.70. The mutation result depends on supervised fine-tuning; zero-shot results are weak. Figure 5 comparisons use 598 eligible cases from 841 test samples. Check length filtering and negative-sampling assumptions before interpreting generalization. [Paper, Figures 2, 4–5 and Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64512-w).

**Supplement.** Figures 1–2 cover loss weights and masking; 3 covers score distributions; 4 and 8 show predicted structures; 5–6 examine pair order; 7 examines sequence identity; 9–10 cover mutation composition and training curves. Tables 1–4 cover compute, structural confidence and baselines. Specific observations:

- Figure 1 explicitly labels the selected ratio **mask:classification = 1:10**. The Results prose reverses that description.
- Figure 2 interchanges fly/worm labels relative to Figure 2 of the paper and supplementary Figure 5; the workbook confirms the mismatch, as detailed below. Its mouse AUPR also does not improve at 15% masking over the unmasked baseline; the broad wording about consistent improvement needs qualification.
- Similar aggregate results after reversing pair order do not establish exact symmetry for every pair.
- Several illustrated complexes have very low ipTM scores. These illustrations do not establish correct interfaces.
- Mutation-model selection uses validation loss, with epoch 19 selected.

Source: [Supplementary Information](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-025-64512-w/MediaObjects/41467_2025_64512_MOESM1_ESM.pdf).

**Peer review.** The revision added TUnA and the Bernett benchmark and substantially rebuilt the mutation analysis. Reviewer 3 explicitly challenged selection of loss weights using the test species. The response retained that choice and described it as a technical benchmark; my reading is that relabelling does not remove the resulting model-selection concern. The authors clarified that structural examples illustrate selected successes and do not demonstrate superiority over structure predictors. Their response about mutation overlap does not establish generalization to entirely unseen protein families. The reviewers ultimately accepted the revisions. [Reports and responses, especially PDF pages 21–28](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-025-64512-w/MediaObjects/41467_2025_64512_MOESM3_ESM.pdf).

**Reporting summary.** This is a computational study using public datasets. Dataset sizes follow availability and preprocessing. The summary documents length exclusions, sampled negatives, and random splitting for the mutation dataset; it does not document a protein-disjoint mutation split. [Reporting Summary, pages 2–3](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41467-025-64512-w/MediaObjects/41467_2025_64512_MOESM2_ESM.pdf).

**Limited code check.** The downloaded current `main` training script applies ReLU to the CLS representation before a linear classifier, uses positive-class weighting in BCE, and defaults to MLM weight 1 and classification weight 10. It currently hard-codes masking probability 0.22, whereas the publication selects 0.15. These are observations about the fetched repository state, not verification of the publication's archived training run. [Training script](https://github.com/liudan111/PLM-interact/blob/main/PLMinteract/train_mlm.py).
